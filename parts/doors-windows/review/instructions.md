# Instructions to the reviewer

These are the instructions that the reviewer gets for every building in this part.

## What to score

- Score **only** the doors and windows that the agent added or changed. Use the [doors and windows standard](standard.md).
- Look at the doors and windows on **every floor** of the building, not only the ground floor or one typical room.
- Do not score room planning, furniture, services, structure, wall types, or problems that were in the starting model.
- Look at the rooms around each opening only to decide if that opening is suitable, fully in its wall, usable and safe.
- Score the facade only as the windows make it.
- Score what the agent says about its work only when it is about the doors and windows.

## What the request does not say

The request is one general sentence, for example "Add doors and windows to this hotel." There is no hidden number of doors or windows, and no hidden door type, size or swing direction. Different designs can all be correct. Accept every design that is coherent.

## What to look at

- **Coverage:** every floor in use. Report missing doors or windows by floor and by room use.
- **Access:** from each room to the corridors, the step from public areas to staff areas, privacy, daylight, and the right type of door or window for each room. The planned room connections of the starting model are context, not an answer key.
- **Boundaries:** tell apart outside walls, walls between rooms, and walls that face an atrium. There must be no upper-floor door into the void of a tall room. Stores without windows, imaging rooms and auditoriums can be correct.
- **Door and window types:** the type must suit the room. A product name alone does not prove this. Check that each opening is fully in its wall, its size, its sill height, and that it does not overlap other openings.
- **How each door and window opens:** the room it opens into, the hinge side, an inward window, and the clear width. Judge this from the measured model, not from a picture.
- **Movement:** an opening door or window must not hit walls, rooms or landings near it. A closed door in a picture does not prove that it can open freely.
- **Claims:** compare what the agent says with the model. When the reviewer cannot confirm something, it writes "not assessed". That is not a pass and not a fault of the agent.

## The starting model

- Keep the starting building as it is.
- The stair and lift zones are reserved spaces that are the same on every floor. They are not real stairs or lifts. Do not reduce the score because the building has no real stairs or lifts.
- Do not say that the result proves a safe escape route or that it obeys building regulations.

The reviewer gives one score from 1 to 10, with notes. This benchmark does not certify that a design obeys building regulations.
